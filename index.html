Guiding principles

  • Easy = actually easy. 5:00–5:15/km, no drift. Every fitness gain comes from the quality sessions — easy runs exist to let you execute them.
  • Two quality days per week from Week 3 onwards (Tue + Thu). Week 1 is a single-session reintroduction; Week 2 is race-oriented.
  • Threshold-heavy, not VO2-heavy. HM PR comes from lifting T-pace, not chasing 400s. VO2 work introduced only in Week 4+.
  • Strides on easy days — 5–6 × 100 m fast. Free adaptation for running economy, no injury risk.
  • Long-run progression peaks 3 weeks out. Week 4 (22km w/ HMP block) is the aerobic-specific peak.
